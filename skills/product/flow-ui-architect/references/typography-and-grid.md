# Typography and grid

## Typography roles

Define semantic roles instead of choosing a font and improvising sizes:

- Display: optional brand or editorial expression.
- Page title: identifies the current job or destination.
- Section title: groups task-relevant content.
- Body: readable instructions and content.
- Label: controls and fields.
- Metadata: secondary context with sufficient contrast.
- Numeric: aligned and tabular when comparison matters.

Choose type by language coverage, readability, platform, brand intent and density. A visual direction may use a serif, humanist sans or grotesk, but the role system must still support scanning and accessible reading.

## Grid and composition

Record shell, content columns, alignment edge, max width, gutters, density mode and responsive transformation. Strong alignment is usually more valuable than decorative composition in operational interfaces. Editorial and expressive pages may vary column rhythm when the content supports it.

Do not use equal columns or centered hero composition without a content reason. Keep content that must be compared on a shared alignment system. Let tables and long labels influence the grid before styling cards.

## Density and stress

Density follows task frequency, content volume and session duration. Compact, standard and relaxed are descriptive modes, not universal numeric quotas. Validate with realistic long labels, large numbers, missing values, localized text and narrow screens.

Review type hierarchy, line height, numeric alignment, wrapping, truncation, focus rings and disabled states at every responsive mode.
