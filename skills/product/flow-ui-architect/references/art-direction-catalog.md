# Art direction catalog

## How to use this catalog

These are visual languages and product directions, not a dropdown of themes. Choose based on the Visual Context Profile, task density and brand intent. A design system such as Material, Fluent, Carbon or Spectrum is a behavior/component foundation; a direction such as Swiss, Editorial or Humanist is a visual language. They may be combined, but they are not interchangeable.

| Direction | Type | Core grammar | Good fit | Main caution | Typical AI failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Swiss / International Typographic | Historical design movement | Explicit grid, left alignment, asymmetric order, restrained color, precise typography | B2B, finance, analysis, docs, editors, professional tools | Can feel too severe for playful products | Lots of whitespace and sans text without a real grid |
| Editorial / Magazine | Editorial language | Strong headline roles, varied columns, photography, rhythm and readable body copy | Media, culture, research, brand stories, content commerce | Poor fit for high-frequency transaction work | Every page becomes a cover with oversized headings |
| Neo-grotesque product | Product direction | Neutral grotesk, compact grid, restrained radius and shadow, structure-first | SaaS, developer tools, collaboration, productivity | Easy to imitate familiar tech brands | Dark gray, purple accent and glow become the whole identity |
| Functional minimalism | Strategy/direction | Few competing elements, clear type, purposeful negative space | Tools, reading, efficiency and long-session products | Do not remove information needed for complex tasks | Low information density and weak affordance called minimalism |
| Modernist / Bauhaus-derived | Historical language translated for UI | Geometry, modularity, asymmetry, strong contrast or primary color | Creative tools, culture, portfolios, experimental brands | Strong expression can undermine serious workflows | Decorative red/yellow/blue blocks with no task role |
| Humanist / warm product | Product direction | Humanist type, warm neutrals, soft but controlled shapes, natural imagery | Health, education, community and lifestyle | Too much softness reduces scan efficiency in dense workbenches | Beige, rounded cards and hand-drawn illustration template |
| Organic / biomorphic | Trend/language | Natural color, irregular curves, texture and flexible composition | Wellness, food, lifestyle and experience products | Poor fit for financial tables and operations | Unrelated blobs and gradients |
| Neo-brutalism | Contemporary trend | Strong borders, hard shadow, direct typography and high contrast | Creative brands, culture, youth and events | Noise can overwhelm hierarchy and risk-sensitive work | Every control gets thick borders and shadows |
| Glass / translucent layering | Material trend or platform layer | Blur, translucency and depth between navigation/control layers | Media, spatial interfaces and OS-like overlays | Avoid dense text, tables, forms and many stacked cards | Every card becomes glass |
| Material / Material Expressive | Product design system | Semantic roles, shape, elevation, motion and broad component coverage | Android-first and cross-platform consumer apps | Default components can look like a demo | Dynamic color and default components without brand direction |
| Fluent-like productivity | Product design system/direction | Neutral hierarchy, layered surfaces, brand token and productivity semantics | Enterprise collaboration and desktop/web productivity | Acrylic or glass without the underlying hierarchy | Decorative translucent surfaces |
| Enterprise dense / Carbon-Ant-like | Product system direction | High density, strong form and table structure, restrained color | B2B, operations, finance, data and infrastructure | Too dense for emotional consumer onboarding | KPI cards replace a real workbench |
| Luxury / premium editorial | Brand language | Photography, fine type pairing, low chroma, controlled accent and slow motion | Luxury, hospitality, automotive, premium property and culture | Does not suit everyday high-frequency operations | Black, gold and serif alone are called luxury |
| Playful / expressive | Product direction | Characterful type, illustration, shape, color and motion | Education, creators, social and younger consumer products | Expression must not hide task hierarchy | Random pills, emoji and shapes |
| Retro / Y2K / pixel / futurist | Cultural trend | Era-specific type, chrome, pixel or scanline treatment, non-standard grid | Events, music, games, campaigns and culture | Keep core workflows legible and stable | Theme skin applied to every control |

For every selection, state which parts are adopted, which are rejected, and why. Never describe a direction as a universal psychological effect.
